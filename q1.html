Welcome to the World of Dogs

The dog is a domesticated descendant of wolves. Also called the domestic dog, it was selectively bred during the Late Pleistocene by hunter-gatherers. Dogs and the modern gray wolf share a common ancestor.Dogs are domesticated members of the family Canidae. They are classified as a species in the Canis genus,alongside wolves (Canis lupus), but have sometimes historically been considered a subspecies of Canis lupus.
